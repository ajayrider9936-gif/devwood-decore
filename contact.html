Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Devwood Dekor showroom located in Sardarshahar?

Our showroom and artisan workshop are located on Taranagar Road, exactly 1 Kilometer from Sardarshahar Circle, Sardarshahar, Rajasthan. You can also call us directly at 9783656009 for live directions.

Can I customize the furniture dimensions and wood polish?

Yes! Because all furniture is handcrafted from solid Sheesham and Teak wood right in our Sardarshahar workshop, we can customize lengths, heights, storage configurations, and finish colors (Honey, Walnut, Natural Teak, or Distressed Antique) to match your home interiors.

How do you ship furniture and antique items safely across India?

Every item is first cocooned in multi-layer shock-absorbent foam, corner protectors, and bubble wrap, followed by custom wooden pallet crates. We partner with specialized furniture transport carriers offering insured door-to-door delivery across all Indian cities.

How do I place an order online?

You can simply browse our Shop Collection, click "Enquire on WhatsApp", or add items to your cart and hit "Confirm Order on WhatsApp". Our team on 9783656009 will immediately assist you with video calls, HD pictures, and payment details.
