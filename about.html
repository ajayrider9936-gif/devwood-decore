Born in Shekhawati

The Art of Sardarshahar Woodcraft

Sardarshahar, located in the historic Churu district of Rajasthan, has long been revered as an epicenter of Indian hardwood craft. For centuries, the royal havelis and merchant palaces of Shekhawati stood adorned with hand-chiseled wooden doors, magnificent arches, and brass-studded timber chests that braved the harsh desert climate with unmatched dignity.

"We do not merely cut and join wood; we breathe royal Rajasthani heritage into every grain of seasoned Sheesham."

At Devwood Dekor, we carry forward this unbroken lineage. Operating from our workshop and showroom on Taranagar Road (just 1 KM from Sardarshahar Circle), our master artisans work with authentic seasoned Sheesham (Indian Rosewood) and Sagwan (Teak Wood) using traditional handheld gouges, chisels, and hand-rubbed wax finishes.

Rare Rajasthani Antiques & Restoration

Beyond newly manufactured solid wood furniture, Devwood Dekor is celebrated as a sanctuary for genuine antique items. From century-old Haveli doors salvaged from royal estates to antique brass lamps, Damchiya bridal chests, and vintage Jharokhas.

Our restoration philosophy preserves the historical soul and weathered patina of every artifact while structurally reinforcing the joints, hinges, and iron latches so they can be admired for another century in modern penthouses and luxury villas.
