The Handcrafting Legacy of Sardarshahar

In the desert heartland of Sardarshahar, Churu, woodcraft is not just an industry—it is an inherited art form passed down over generations. Every piece at Devwood Dekor begins with carefully seasoned solid Sheesham (Indian Rosewood) and premium Teak timber.

Our karigars transform heavy hardwood timber into graceful, hand-chiseled palace furniture, jaali lattices, and painstakingly restored antiques that withstand decades of memories.

  • Kiln-seasoned & anti-termite treated timber
  • Authentic hand-carving without modern composite pressed wood
  • Heritage brass work inlaid by hereditary metalsmiths
